Colour a page right here

Pick a page from any of the three books and colour it in your browser, on a computer, tablet or phone. Every art supply behaves like the real thing:

  • Pencil crayons and wax crayons that catch the paper's grain
  • Watercolours that dry with soft, darker edges
  • Felt tips and alcohol markers that layer and blend
  • Glitter gel pens, a graphite pencil, a paint bucket and an eraser

Little hands can switch on Stay inside the lines. When you're done, save the picture or print it for the fridge.
