Every few years, four animal communities of the Wildwoods gather for competition and games, to test their strengths and abilities. These competitions are known as the Wildwood Games!

The Games open when wise old **Snappy Snapsalot**, the snapping turtle who referees every event, lights the ceremonial torch. Then each team shows what it does best:

- **The Wolves** love speed: 100, 200 and 800 metre races, relays and the long jump.
- **The Eagles** love finesse: sky-ribbon dancing, sky-basketball and archery.
- **The Foxes** love agility: soccer, the steeplechase, target practice and kayaking.
- **The Bears** love strength: weightlifting and wrestling, and a balance-beam event nobody expected them to win.

Then the teams meet in mixed events: a honeycomb-eating contest, soccer, tug-of-war, rope climbing, diving and swimming, and finally a moon-lit marathon through the forest. Medals are handed out, fireworks light up the closing ceremony, and the very last picture is a trophy made just for the reader.

Who will you cheer for?
